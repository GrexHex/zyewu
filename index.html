Process
01
Brief
We listen, ask questions and take notes about your goals, your people and your ideas.
02
Quote
A clear quote split into parts, with no surprises and no commitment.
03
Research
References, similar projects, very different projects, colours, shapes and marks. Everything we need to find a direction.
04
Sketches
Pen and paper first. We throw down every idea, pick a few and tidy them into a short presentation.
05
Revisions
We work on the chosen version together until it feels right.
06
Delivery
Every file in the formats you need, organised so the next step is easy.
07
Cheers
We celebrate a finished project, post something about it and raise a glass.
