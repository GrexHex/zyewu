PIPBLOCK
/ Typeface

PIPBLOCK is a modular display typeface derived from the structural logic of a six-sided die. Built on a grid, it turns pips, edges and geometric forms into a controlled typographic language, shown here as a booklet and specimen posters. Replace this with your own project story.
